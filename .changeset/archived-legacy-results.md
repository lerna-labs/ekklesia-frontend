Results for a closed ballot from the earlier off-chain voting flow now show an "Archived result" notice that says voting is closed and the tally was counted off chain and not recorded on chain, in place of the in-progress and Hydra-final wording. The proposals page status line for such a ballot says the same. Where a ballot has a pass threshold, each proposal shows a "Threshold reached" or "Threshold not reached" badge, with the rule (Yes above the threshold share of Yes and No, Abstain not counted) in its accessible text, on the proposals list, the proposal page and the results page. The proposals list gains a Reached and Not reached filter.
