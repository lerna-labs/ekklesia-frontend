Results for a closed ballot from the earlier off-chain voting flow now show an "Archived result" notice. It says voting is closed, and the tally was counted off chain and not recorded on chain. It replaces the in-progress and Hydra-final wording. The proposals page status line for such a ballot says the same. Such a ballot no longer shows the PROVISIONAL badge on the proposals page or the results page, and no longer lists an "On-Chain Results: Pending" row.
